# Backlog

Ide di sini belum menjadi scope. Promosi ke sprint memerlukan REQ/ADR, priority, dependencies, acceptance criteria dan kapasitas.

## Post-v1 candidates

- Dedicated courier role/app dan route optimization.
- Multi-outlet/multi-tenant.
- WhatsApp/email/push notification.
- Production payment/refund readiness.
- Loyalty, promo, subscription.
- Inventory/POS/IoT scale.
- Multilingual/dark mode.
- Advanced fraud/risk scoring dan forecasting.

## Item template

`ID | problem/outcome | requester | evidence | scope | dependencies | risk/privacy | acceptance | priority | decision status`.

Tidak boleh mengerjakan item backlog hanya karena mudah/menarik.
